# Stage 3 river resident NPC direction study

The first major NPC needs a species/job silhouette distinct from the low quadruped capybara player and a function tied to repairing the water community. Four **text-only original first-round concepts** were made: a beaver bridge/sluice craftsperson (A), an otter flow researcher (B), a heron wetland gardener (C), and a turtle ecology record keeper (D). Exact prompts, raw/candidate SHA-256 and limitations are in the [first-round review](../../art/candidates/npc_river_residents_v001/REVIEW.md) and [candidate manifest](../../art/candidates/npc_river_residents_v001/CANDIDATE_MANIFEST.csv).

At individually normalized 144px height, A's tail, work apron and survey map read together as a practical bridgewright; D's shell and journal also read, but its broad ground-level footprint is closer to the player's. B is slimmer but too portrait-like and eye-level; C is striking at native size but its long legs and botanical basket are thin at game size. **A is a provisional direction for a targeted second round, D a separate later-NPC backup.** This does not select an NPC master or name, imply a schedule/storyline, or approve any runtime sprite.

Second-round art should use A's exact project-owned image as its declared parent and improve the elevated camera, body/leg structure, painted surface simplification at game scale, tail connection, apron/map stability and upper-left light. Keep an editable four-direction anatomy and color/prop sheet in scope from the start. After that, compare 8-direction turns, idle/walk and the accessories in actual home-scale GPU frames. The current first-round images must remain in `art/candidates` and must never be copied into `game/assets` as character art without the full promotion gate.
