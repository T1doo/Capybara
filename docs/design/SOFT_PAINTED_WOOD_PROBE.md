# Stage 3 shared painted-wood study

The house, bridge, dock and waterwheel should read as one painted world. The bridge and dock were sampling the same original material atlas at full local contrast, making near-camera knots and short repeated grain more prominent than the surrounding forms. This batch keeps that project-owned atlas unchanged and adds a shared code-native [`soft_painted_wood.gdshader`](../../game/assets/shaders/soft_painted_wood.gdshader) to the bridge, dock and waterwheel Polygon2D timber pieces. The shader retains 72% of sampled texture color and blends the remainder toward one warm wood wash, preserving each polygon's tint and source Alpha.

Actual Compatibility GPU fixed-view captures are in ignored `build/art-pipeline/bridge_review_softwood_20260923`, `dock_review_softwood_20260923` and `waterwheel_review_softwood_20260923`; the earlier `bridge_review`, `dock_review` and `waterwheel_review` folders remain as before images. Two deliberately narrow board patches changed from about **18.33 to 13.82** (bridge) and **18.42 to 13.76** (dock) in grayscale standard deviation. This confirms local contrast softening, not that all wood or the complete composition is visually approved. Motion/reduced-motion waterwheel views were captured with the same existing fixture and ran without renderer errors.

The repeated large knots are still visible, as are the straight board silhouettes and simplified posts/ropes. No new wood grain source, more varied boards, geometry pass, hand-authored breakups, house/dock/bridge material harmony review, or three-round whole-scene assessment has been completed. ENV3-001 remains open/in progress; this shader is a technical step toward its acceptance gate, not formal environment art approval.
