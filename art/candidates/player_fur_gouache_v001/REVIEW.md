## Decision and limits

Keep **technical_unapproved**. It is a usable source experiment, not an accepted fur material or a new character master. A single repeating pigment tile cannot supply the authored face/cheek planes, purposeful directional fur, cloth detail and silhouette required by the art bible. Stop cycling global noise/texture intensity as the sole fix; the next iteration must address the character form and region-specific painting together. The existing camera and foot-contact calibration remain useful.

No back/side material seam review, eight directions, animated texture swimming check, production texture bake/atlas, import-size budget, independent art approval or final integration has passed. Alpha/sourcing and a technical renderer pass do not close AB-VIS-003/004. The generated original is retained via Git LFS; rendered `.blend` files, PNG comparisons and GPU screenshots are local ignored build evidence and must be regenerated on a clean checkout.

Reproduction uses the pinned Blender binary with `--background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/art/probe_player_pigment_v001.py`, then `--model-manifest`, `--candidate-manifest` and a new `--output-dir` under `build`. Optional `--paint-scale`, `--soft-weight`, `--full-weight` preserve the comparison settings above. Use the existing `capture_character_candidate.gd` with the generated render manifest for the same real-GPU home comparison.
