Show the FULL BODY in a single elevated 35-degree down-right three-quarter view, clearly separate from the ground, with a broad flat beaver tail visible to one side, a compact sturdy torso, short legs, small dark eyes, rounded muzzle, subtle natural beaver incisors, and a pair of dexterous animal paws. This NPC must have a distinct upright-working silhouette, clearly different from the game's low, long quadruped capybara player. Give the beaver a muted blue-grey waxed canvas work apron with two simple pockets, a rolled paper survey map held in one paw, and a small wooden measuring tool tucked safely into the apron; no weapon. Warm russet-brown fur, calm capable adult expression, painterly brush texture and hand-worked edges, sophisticated non-pixel illustrated storybook 2D, readable at 144-pixel in-game character height. Soft fixed upper-left key light, mild 35-degree orthographic camera, no exaggerated baby eyes, no plastic 3D gloss, no human fingers, no extra limbs, no oversized hat, no brand or recognizable character/IP reference. Genuine transparent RGBA background with generous clear padding and no checkerboard, ground, cast shadow, text, labels, logo, signature or watermark. This is an unapproved original concept exploration, not a turnaround sheet or production animation.
```
