# First-round major river NPC concept prompts

The four first-round calls used built-in `image_gen.imagegen` with **no referenced image paths** and no CLI/API key. The targeted second-round call `NPC-A2` used **only** the project-owned candidate `NPC-A` (`npc_bridgewright_a_v001.png`, SHA-256 `bc0fa71aad72ef98cac48ace04bee2233dfb0cca9c9f96dfd5cf4e0f26faf759`) as its edit reference. Exact model name was not disclosed. No call used the uploaded quarantined or copyrighted character imagery as a reference.

## NPC-A

```text
Create one entirely original 2D illustrated character concept for the Capybara cozy water-restoration game: an adult BEAVER riverworks craftsperson, a kind but practical bridge and sluice repairer. Show the FULL BODY in a single elevated 35-degree down-right three-quarter view, clearly separate from the ground, with a broad flat beaver tail visible to one side, a compact sturdy torso, short legs, small dark eyes, rounded muzzle, subtle natural beaver incisors, and a pair of dexterous animal paws. This NPC must have a distinct upright-working silhouette, clearly different from the game's low, long quadruped capybara player. Give the beaver a muted blue-grey waxed canvas work apron with two simple pockets, a rolled paper survey map held in one paw, and a small wooden measuring tool tucked safely into the apron; no weapon. Warm russet-brown fur, calm capable adult expression, painterly brush texture and hand-worked edges, sophisticated non-pixel illustrated storybook 2D, readable at 144-pixel in-game character height. Soft fixed upper-left key light, mild 35-degree orthographic camera, no exaggerated baby eyes, no plastic 3D gloss, no human fingers, no extra limbs, no oversized hat, no brand or recognizable character/IP reference. Genuine transparent RGBA background with generous clear padding and no checkerboard, ground, cast shadow, text, labels, logo, signature or watermark. This is an unapproved original concept exploration, not a turnaround sheet or production animation.
```

## NPC-B

```text
Create ONE completely original full-body concept for an animal NPC in a sophisticated non-pixel 2D storybook water-restoration game. Option B: an adult RIVER OTTER who studies water flow, clearly unlike a low quadruped capybara player or a stout beaver craftsperson. Slender elongated S-curve torso, long tapered otter tail, short rounded ears, narrow natural muzzle, small calm dark eyes, compact paws and short sturdy legs; an alert but gentle working adult, not a baby mascot. Elevated orthographic 35-degree down-right three-quarter view, one consistent upper-left key light. A restrained slate-green woven shoulder cape with a visible one-sided fastener, a small waterproof field notebook and a corked water sample vial in a belt pouch; no weapon, no boots, no logo. Distinct silhouette readable when reduced to 144-pixel body height. Original painterly gouache-like brush surfaces with subtle warm umber/silver-brown fur and moss/river-green fabric, convincing anatomy and functional fabric. Preserve a genuine transparent RGBA background with generous padding; no checkerboard, ground, cast shadow, vignette, text, labels, signature or watermark. No resemblance to known cartoon/IP characters or any particular artist. This is a single unapproved first-round concept, not a turnaround, sprite sheet or animation.
```

## NPC-C

Mature warm attentive character, restrained eyes and believable proportions; not a cute mascot. True transparent RGBA background and ample empty padding, no ground/cast shadow, checkerboard, text, letters, labels, logo, signature or watermark. No likeness to any known entertainment character/IP or particular artist. This is an unapproved first-round single concept, not a turnaround or animation sheet.
